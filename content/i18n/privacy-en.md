연심(連心) (hereinafter "this site") takes your privacy seriously and provides the following privacy policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

This site has no account registration and does not collect information that directly identifies an individual, such as names or email addresses. However, the following information may be collected automatically for service operation and ad delivery.

- Visit records through cookies and similar technologies
- Environment information such as browser type, device type, and operating system
- Approximate location information such as the country and city of access
- Referral sources and page usage statistics

## 2. Use of Cookies

This site uses cookies to improve the browsing experience, analyze visit statistics, and deliver ads. You may refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings; in that case, some features may be limited.

## 3. Google AdSense

This site displays ads through Google AdSense.

- Google may use advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve personalized ads based on your visits to this site and other websites.
- You may opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

For more information on Google's advertising and privacy practices, please see the [Google Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to collect the following visit statistics for service improvement.

- **Visit statistics** : page views, sessions, time of access
- **Referral sources** : direct access, search, external links, and so on
- **Environment information** : device type, OS, browser, resolution
- **Regional information** : country and city

No personally identifiable information (such as IP addresses) is collected in this process, and the collected data is not used for any purpose other than service improvement.

## 5. Provision to Third Parties

Except where required by law, this site does not sell or provide user information to third parties. Automatic collection by the advertising and analytics services above follows each provider's own policies.

## 6. Contact

For inquiries regarding this privacy policy, please use the contact methods listed on the [About](/about/) page.
