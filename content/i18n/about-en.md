## About 연심(連心)

<strong>연심(連心)</strong> is an **unofficial fan reference site** that collects Identity, E.G.O, and Gift information for **Limbus Company**, a game by [Project Moon](https://www.projectmoon.studio/).

It reorganizes the stats, skills, passives, and resistances scattered across the game into tables and cards, aiming to help players compare Identities and E.G.O and manage their Mirror Dungeon gifts.

## Features

- **Gift page** — Mirror Dungeon E.G.O Gift checklist with search and filters
- **Sinner page** — Detailed Identity and E.G.O information for all 12 Sinners (skills, passives, resistances, changes by sync level, and more)
- Advanced search by keyword and by sin / physical resistance
- Convenience features such as a support passive overview and card image copying
